==================================================
MODULE 2 - TOPIC 4: dSOURCES
==================================================

--------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IS A dSOURCE?
--------------------------------------------------
A dSource is a copy of the source database that is compressed and stored
in the Delphix Engine, along with all incremental updates. It is used to
make virtual copies of your data.

--------------------------------------------------
2. LINKING dSOURCES
--------------------------------------------------
A Linked dSource within the Continuous Data Engine ingests data from the
Source Environment(s) and creates a dSource object on the engine.

--------------------------------------------------
3. ENABLING AND DISABLING dSOURCES
--------------------------------------------------
* Some operations, such as dSource upgrade, are NOT available unless the
  dSource is disabled.
* Disabling a dSource turns off communication between the Delphix Engine
  and the source database, but it does NOT remove the configuration.
* When a disabled dSource is later enabled, it resumes communication and
  incremental data updates from the source database according to the
  original policies and data management configurations.

--------------------------------------------------
4. STEPS TO DISABLE A dSOURCE (DASHBOARD)
--------------------------------------------------
Step 1 - Find the Datasets menu.
Step 2 - Choose a dSource to disable.
Step 3 - Open the dSource options.
Step 4 - Confirm the Disable action.
Step 5 - Review the status of the dSource.

--------------------------------------------------
5. DISABLING IS A PREREQUISITE FOR
--------------------------------------------------
1) Database migration
2) Upgrading the dSource metadata after the upgrade of the associated
   data source
3) Restoring the source database from a backup
