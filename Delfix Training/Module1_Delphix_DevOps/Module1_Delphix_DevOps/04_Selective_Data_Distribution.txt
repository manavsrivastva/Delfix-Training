==================================================
MODULE 1 - TOPIC 4: SELECTIVE DATA DISTRIBUTION (SDD)
==================================================

--------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IS SDD?
--------------------------------------------------
Selective Data Distribution permits the distribution of MASKED data
between Delphix Engines.

* Configured on the source Delphix Engine or in the Data Control Tower.
* First copies a subset of masked VDBs to a target Delphix Engine.
* Then sends incremental updates, either manually or on a schedule.

--------------------------------------------------
2. HOW SDD PROTECTS SENSITIVE DATA
--------------------------------------------------
* DxFS (Delphix File System) uses SDD to redact sensitive data from the
  replication stream.
* Only masked or permitted data is replicated to the Non-Production
  datacenter.
* Sensitive data from the dSource NEVER leaves the Production engine.
* This gives protection at both the VDB layer and the replication layer.

--------------------------------------------------
3. IMPORTANT NOTE
--------------------------------------------------
Only MASKED VDBs can be added to a Selective Data Distribution spec and
replicated to another engine outside the production zone (dev/test
on-premise or in the cloud).

--------------------------------------------------
4. SDD vs. REPLICATION
--------------------------------------------------
SDD is not the only way to replicate data in Delphix. It is one strategy
within the broader replication architecture (see Topic 3 - Delphix
Replication).
