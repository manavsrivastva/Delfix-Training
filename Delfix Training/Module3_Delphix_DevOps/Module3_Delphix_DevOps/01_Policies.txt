==================================================
MODULE 3 - TOPIC 1: POLICIES
==================================================

--------------------------------------------------
1. WHY POLICIES?
--------------------------------------------------
Creating policies is a great way to automate the management of datasets in
the Delphix Engine. Whether for syncing data, taking snapshots, or
refreshing virtual databases, policies ensure that data is ready when
needed.

The Delphix policies area lets Delphix Administrators control and
guarantee behaviors within the Delphix Engines.

--------------------------------------------------
2. THE 5 CATEGORIES OF POLICIES
--------------------------------------------------
The Continuous Data Engine uses 5 categories of policies in conjunction
with database objects (they control when and how snapshots and SnapSync
operations run):

1) SnapSync
   - How often snapshots of a source database are taken for a dSource.

2) VDB Snapshot
   - How often snapshots are taken for a VDB.

3) Retention
   - How long snapshots and log files are retained for dSources and VDBs.

4) VDB Refresh
   - Automatic scheduled refreshes of VDBs.
   - The default setting for this policy is "None".

5) Replica Retention
   - How long snapshots are retained on replicated namespaces for dSources
     and VDBs after they have been deleted on the replication source.

--------------------------------------------------
3. DELPHIX POLICY TYPES
--------------------------------------------------
* Default
  - Exist at the domain level and are applied across all objects in a
    category.
  - Managed by users with Delphix Admin credentials.

* Custom
  - Can only be applied to a SPECIFIC database object.
  - Cannot be saved for use with other objects.
  - Managed by users with Delphix Admin credentials, group owners, and
    object owners.

--------------------------------------------------
4. POLICY DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------
SnapSync Policies:
  * Determine how often snapshots of the source database are taken.
  * Can only be applied to dSources.

VDB Refresh Policies:
  * Can only be applied to VDBs.
  * Only used when the user needs to re-provision VDBs from their source
    at regular intervals.

Replica Retention Policies:
  * Define how long snapshots are retained on replicated namespaces for
    dSources and VDBs, after they have been deleted on the replication
    source.
